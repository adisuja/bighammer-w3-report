04

Recommendations — Fixing the Funnel Leakage

  1. Send all reminders from the WebinarGeek email handle — confirmation, 1-day, 1-hour and going-live — and keep syncing to GoHighLevel for tracking. (Reminders currently fire from GHL and open well — 1-hour reminder: 460 delivered, 46% open, 34% click — but registrants get no WebinarGeek calendar hold or single consistent join link. Consolidate the sending on WebinarGeek, the data in GHL.)
  2. Add WhatsApp as a reminder channel (via Twilio) — 1-day, 1-hour and going-live, alongside email. For a late-evening webinar to a cold list, a channel that gets read beats another email.
  3. Stop driving traffic to the LinkedIn event page — always use the main landing page. The LinkedIn event page doesn't feed WebinarGeek: ~35 people registered on the W3 LinkedIn event, but only 1 completed an actual webinar registration. Point every LinkedIn touch at dataopsmasterclass.com so the sign-up is captured and tracked.
  4. Add traffic channels beyond cold email:
    • LinkedIn organic content — build a founder/company content cadence.
    • LinkedIn outreach sequences — templates ready.
    • AI-personalised cold emails — V2 sequence with a personalisation step.
    • Newsletter nurture campaign — warm the list between webinars.
  5. Make the webinar content more actionable. Expand the 3-step framework across ~3 more slides with concrete, do-this-now steps — the retention curve above shows heavy end-of-webinar drop-off, where at least half should stay to reach the CTA.
